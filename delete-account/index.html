Delete your Remeder account

Remeder · app.remeder.mobile · Last updated 21 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Remeder account and everything stored with it, what is removed, and what to do if you can no longer sign in.

Deletion is permanent. Your medicines, schedules, and dose history cannot be recovered afterwards. If you want to keep a record, use Profile → Privacy & security → Export first — it writes your data to a file you can save or send to yourself.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Remeder and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Privacy & security.
  4. Choose Delete account.
  5. Sign in again when asked — this confirms it is really you.
  6. Type DELETE when prompted, then choose Delete permanently.

The wipe starts immediately and cannot be cancelled once confirmed. From that moment no device can add new data to the account, even if it is still signed in elsewhere.

If you can no longer sign in

If you have uninstalled the app, lost access to your email address, or cannot complete the steps above, email privacy@remeder.app from the address on the account and ask for deletion.

We will confirm your request, verify that the address matches the account, and complete the deletion. Expect a reply within 30 days. Do not send your password — we will never ask for it.

What is kept, and for how long

A record that the account was deleted is kept indefinitely. It contains no medical data and no personal details beyond the internal account identifier. We keep it because it is what continues to refuse writes from a device whose sign-in token outlived the account, and because those identifiers are never reused.

If someone shared a person with you and you recorded doses for them, those dose records stay in that person's history, under your name. They are part of someone else's medication record, which belongs to the account that keeps it.

Remeder's server keeps ordinary request logs, which Google Cloud deletes after 30 days.

Nothing else is retained. Remeder has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services, so there is no copy of your data with a third party to delete separately. Ordinary infrastructure backups age out on their normal cycle and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

Deleting part of your data instead

You do not have to delete the whole account to remove something. Inside the app you can delete an individual medicine, a whole course, or an entire profile, and each is removed straight away. Profile details can also be edited at any time.
